# How Title reconciles supplied claims

Title reads the three exact [operator exports](INPUTS.md), verifies current file/source authority, and checks instrument identity, order, scope and required inventory. It applies declared opening balances and conveyances separately for record title and operating rights in each explicit depth band.

Shares remain exact fractions. With WI `1/2`, royalty `1/4` of WI and ORRI `1/16` of the whole, NRI is `5/16`. If the ORRI is instead `1/16` of WI, NRI is `11/32`. Those are arithmetic under supplied terms, not ownership claims established from a county name or description. Missing or unknown burdens are not zero.

The employee proposes its own observations/questions. The backend validates exact section quotes, byte offsets/hashes, required question/instrument coverage and canonical work. Missing inputs, conflicts or unknown post-term holding remain blockers. A separate human inspects the exact packet and installed qualification before private recording. Normalization, source coverage, authenticity, legal validity and professional acceptance remain outside these technical checks.
