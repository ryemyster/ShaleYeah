# Title initial source and label evidence

The fictional normalized instruments and 28 initial labels were authored by
Codex under the maintainer's standing authorization for the refactor. They were
frozen before Title backend, grader and prompt implementation.
`label-freeze.json` records the source/dataset hashes and timing.

The BLM transfer handout's first two pages motivate separate interest kinds and
depth scope. BLM's record-coverage note motivates a bounded inventory, and the
Texas RRC jurisdiction page motivates refusal of permit-based title clearance.
Exact URLs, retrieval dates and limited uses are in `source-oracles.json`.
The named parties, tract, recordings and contractual terms here are invented.
No filing fee, federal procedure or jurisdiction-wide title rule is encoded.

The expected fractions were checked with Python `fractions.Fraction`, without
importing the Title implementation. For WI 1/2 and royalty 1/4 of WI, a 1/16
whole-relative ORRI gives NRI 5/16; a 1/16 WI-relative ORRI gives 11/32.
The two calculations use different supplied terms. Neither is a regional default.

Failed dataset cases represent an incorrect completed claim or unauthorized
action under changed inputs, source evidence or runtime observations. Correct
blocked/deferred responses are tested separately at the executing boundary.
Generated tool observations and actual native traces are evidence of execution;
they must not replace these independent initial arithmetic and authority labels.

These are provisional technical fixtures. They do not verify original-record
authenticity, OCR/extraction accuracy, county coverage, legal validity, title
marketability, professional correctness or real operator effort. A qualified
reviewer may refine labels in a new recorded version. Retain prior versions and
results; fixture passes never grant professional acceptance or title clearance.

`observed-packet.json` and `observed-variants.json` are actual synthetic backend
captures, bound by `observations-manifest.json`. `provider-proposal.json` is
provider test prose whose citations must be obtained through real quote lookup.
Neither changes the original expected fractions. The runner maps the frozen
negative `failed` label to the shared hard-gate `blocked` result without changing
original labels, required failures or hashes. See `docs/EVALUATIONS.md` for the
qualification and human-acceptance distinction.

This installed evaluation fixture copy uses a flat title-performance.json; no employee or backend import is needed by the independent metrics.
