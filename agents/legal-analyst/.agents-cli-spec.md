- Identify the requested question, asset/activity, exact jurisdiction/territory
  and legal as-of date; keep retrieval time separate from legal effective dates.
- Identify each source's actual revision, issuer, source type, status, effective
  interval and section/paragraph. Do not infer applicability from a state name.
- Keep contract text, primary requirements, guidance and counsel interpretations
  distinguishable. Record source rights and any private/privileged restrictions.
- Locate exact source text for each cited requirement, exception or contradiction;
  preserve unanswered questions and missing or conflicting evidence.
- Freeze independently authored expected behavior, initial label author/rationale,

  changing the prompt. Include control, edge and authority cases.
- Prove actual returned work, current source rights, restart, human revision,

### Current implementation gaps

The current backend derives jurisdiction risk, 3/6/12-month approval estimates,
compliance cost bands and blanket permit lists without supplied legal sources.
Its LLM wrappers silently return those defaults on failure. Contract terms are
classified by keywords, without source clauses or contract revisions. The ADK

### Architecture and scope

Selected: **Stand-alone Agent with Progressive Disclosure (Skills)**. One native
ADK/Python specialist retrieves authorized material and proposes a cited
requirement/exception memo. The deterministic MCP backend owns input/citation
validation, canonical work projection and the installed shared review protocol.
It does not replace legal reasoning with an operator's prewritten memo.

Not selected: hierarchical workers, an ADK graph, ambient triggers or a
capability arbitrator. The existing backend business-state adapter enforces the
review transitions; that does not make the agent an ADK graph. No second role,
coordinator, universal legal-rule engine, paid connector or production deploy is
part of this issue.

Reuse the installed contracts, MCP client, employee context/provider/events,
review and evaluation helpers. Use the approved native dependency family already
qualified by the adopted roles; do not upgrade the repository globally.

### Inputs and useful work

Use exact JSON source-profile exports for (1) the matter/questions, jurisdiction,
activity and as-of date, (2) the versioned document/section corpus, and (3) the
operator's review scope/checklist and declared source conflicts. Real operator
files are the first working path; normalized public/internal API snapshots use
the existing source-hook pattern. Vendor-native PDF interpretation and paid
records remain unsupported unless separately qualified.

The agent reads the actual source sections, compares supplied contract clauses
and requirements, and proposes cited requirements, exceptions, contradictions
and unanswered questions. Backend tools validate the proposal against current
source bytes/metadata and derive the canonical memo/work product. A supplied
checklist defines questions and coverage, not a prefilled answer or executable
legal rule. Legal interpretation remains an unreviewed proposal for counsel.

Proposed tools: `read_legal_inputs`, `read_legal_source`, `read_legal_provision`,
`cite_legal_source`,
`get_context_source_metadata`, `compare_legal_sources`, `draft_legal_memo`,
`prepare_legal_review`, `legal_review_status` and `record_legal_memo`.
Source reads are bounded; tool arguments cannot choose credentials, principals,
arbitrary URLs or output paths. No model-facing approval, signature or filing tool.

Require exact revision/section/quote bindings. Use explicit UTF-8 byte spans and
hashes so Python/TypeScript agree on Unicode citations. Jurisdiction/activity
identifiers and date intervals are explicit source assertions, not inferred legal
hierarchies. Declare interval boundaries; unknown dates/status/applicability must
surface as a gap. A page update/retrieval timestamp is not an effective date.

Provision paging and quote lookup are separate queries: the agent selects and
interprets source text, while the backend computes exact byte offsets and hashes.
This avoids asking the model to perform cryptographic or encoding calculations.

Distinguish a complete draft issue memo from legal clearance: an exception or
question can be faithfully reported for counsel, while an unsupported affirmative
requirement cannot become a verified conclusion. Missing/stale/denied sources,
wrong revisions, incomplete question coverage and fabricated citations cannot
produce a ready work product. Preserve document conflicts rather than silently
choosing the latest source as a legal interpretation.

### Human, privacy and execution boundary

An independently enrolled legal reviewer inspects the exact source, memo, context
and evaluation revisions, requests changes, rejects or approves a private advisory
record. The existing review/qualification components enforce current authority,
revision binding and replay protection at the executing backend. Enrollment is
operator-attested authority, not automated verification of a bar license.

Private recording does not grant legal clearance, contract/redline approval,
signature, waiver, filing, settlement or regulatory-submission authority. Those
remain with qualified humans and are outside the model tool surface. Retrieved
text and optional judges cannot grant authority or relax mandatory gates.

Authorized source excerpts may enter the scoped model context only under current
operator rights and configured provider/retention policy. Keep secrets outside
prompts and artifacts. Preserve source/asset/task/principal isolation; no raw
legal output or privileged material is promoted into shared knowledge.

### Initial primary-source basis and limits

- [FERC's responsibilities and limits](https://www.ferc.gov/what-ferc-does), page
  updated 2025-06-18, illustrates why a regulator name alone is not an applicable
  construction/operating approval. The source distinguishes oil transport regulation
  from oil pipeline construction. Use this for a jurisdiction/authority boundary,
  not a general permitting conclusion.
- [EPA's Class II well guidance](https://www.epa.gov/uic/class-ii-oil-and-gas-related-injection-wells)
  and [EPA's application completeness checklist](https://www.epa.gov/uic/uic-class-ii-permit-application-completeness-review-checklist)
  distinguish well classes, program authority and the scope of EPA-administered
  application review. A complete supplied document set is not permit issuance.
- [Official 2022 Michigan Class II primacy rule](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2022-07-28/pdf/2022-16017.pdf),
  87 FR 45251, document 2022-16017: a historical effective-date boundary of
  2022-08-29 with explicit territorial/well-class exceptions. Initial historical
  cases must preserve those exceptions; this is not a claim about all current
  Michigan permitting law or an individual asset's authorization.
- [OFR/GPO legal-status guidance](https://www.ecfr.gov/reader-aids/government-policy-and-ofr-procedures/about-this-site)
  and the [point-in-time reader aid](https://www.ecfr.gov/reader-aids/using-ecfr)
  support versioned source selection and verification against official editions.
  Do not present a guidance webpage or a newer revision as the law for every date.

Use synthetic contracts and explicit, short source excerpts/normalized fixture
records. Preserve provenance and distinguish original quotations from authored
synthetic text. Codex authors the provisional labels under standing authorization;
counsel/contributors may refine them. No measured field accuracy, professional
certification or universal legal correctness is asserted.
