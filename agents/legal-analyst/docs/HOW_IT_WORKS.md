# How Legal Analyst works

1. Discover the exact permitted matter, document revisions and review checklist.
2. Read relevant provisions and compare their declared jurisdiction, territory, activity, status and effective intervals.
3. Select exact quotations with `cite_legal_source`; the backend calculates UTF-8 byte spans and SHA-256.
4. Propose requirements, exceptions, questions and conflicts. Submit that proposal to `draft_legal_memo` for canonical work and source/coverage validation.
5. Return the complete `work` and `legal` objects or an explicit deferral. Before review preparation, verify current backend work again and bind host-owned context.
6. A separately enrolled human inspects the exact private packet and requests changes, rejects or approves. Installed qualification and current source rights precede recording; changed sources require fresh drafting/review.

The agent performs the proposed analysis. The backend verifies source assertions and citations rather than supplying a prewritten operator memo. A valid quotation does not establish legal entailment, professional acceptance or legal clearance. See [evaluation](EVALUATIONS.md) and [integration](INTEGRATION.md).
