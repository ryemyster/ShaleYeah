### Understanding and qualified slice

The role must turn current, reviewed specialist uncertainty and operator risk
policy into a reproducible risk register/scenario packet for a human risk owner.
Its existing ADK wrapper and shape tests do not establish this workflow.
Local source inspection found fixed missing-data risk scores, default confidence,
an unseeded financial proxy labeled NPV/IRR, fixed correlation values and
model-selected file writes. Those paths cannot supply qualification evidence.
Two bounded Context Engine investigations stopped at their iteration limit;
their partial reports are not conclusive audits. Local source reads establish
the observations above.

Primary mode remains **Stand-alone Agent with Progressive Disclosure (Skills)**.
One specialist loads source discovery and exact detail on demand. No Hierarchical
(Orchestrator-Worker), primary Graph-Based Workflow (ADK 2.0), Ambient
(Event-Driven), or new Capability-First arbitrator is selected. The backend owns
the durable deterministic business review state machine. A real need for delegated
roles or background triggers would require a separate approved scope.

The initial qualified method is complete, explicit **joint discrete scenarios**.
It consumes source-bound assumed scenario probabilities and monetary loss
outcomes, with declared currency, horizon and dependency basis. It enumerates
those scenarios exactly; a random seed is not applicable to this deterministic
method. It must not claim Monte Carlo, inferred independence, calibrated field
probabilities, financial NPV/IRR, or professional risk acceptance. Requests for
unsupported sampling or incomplete dependence inputs defer. New financial
solvers, vendor models and a general stochastic platform are outside this issue.

### Inputs, outputs and tool boundary

- Bind exactly three normalized JSON exports: a risk register with specialist
  evidence references, an explicit operator risk policy, and complete joint
  scenarios. Reuse source profile 0.2.0 and employee work/context profile 0.1.0.
  Preserve exact bytes, scope, task/source revisions, as-of time, rights,
  observation/assumption labels and declared limits. The local operator attests
  upstream export rights and review; the adapter must not claim independent
  certification of upstream professional truth.
- Register entries retain identities, category, consequences, mitigation owner,
  mitigation state, conflicts, missing evidence and explicit severity basis.
  Policy supplies required coverage and escalation/acceptance limits. Missing
  evidence or unknown severity cannot become a numeric default or zero risk.
- Scenarios name the risks that occur jointly and the total residual loss for
  that state, rather than silently adding overlapping marginal consequences.
  Validate complete probability mass, identities, units/horizon, nonnegative
  loss and consistent references. Preserve literal zero and unknown separately.
- Report weighted expected loss, explicitly defined weighted-CDF quantiles,
  downside probabilities, severe/unmitigated coverage and actual dependency
  assumptions. Any indicator correlations must be calculated from the declared
  joint distribution and labeled assumed; zero variance is unavailable. Do not
  publish fixed correlations or universal jurisdiction/formation risk scores.
- Expose bounded source discovery/detail, calculation/comparison/drafting,
  exact review preparation/status and a protected private record operation.
  Human decisions are separate enrolled API/CLI operations, never model tools.
  Replace displaced unqualified role paths and old caller-selected writes in
  this PR; preserve any useful validated mathematical helper only with owning
  tests and a clear non-authority contract. No unrelated backend rewrite.

### Context, providers and human authority

Use the accepted installed runtime, client, provider/event and review helpers.
The native role factory owns persona/tool mapping and its retained source/work/
pending-review context. Explicit Gemini/Anthropic profiles replace the latest-model
alias; no global provider/CLI upgrade or mandatory cloud service is authorized.
Credentials remain private host references. Raw model output cannot promote
itself to retained/shared knowledge.

Permitted source retrieval, scenario calculations and drafts may run within
current grants. An enrolled human risk owner inspects exact source, policy,
assumption, work/context/evaluation/action revisions, requests changes, rejects
or approves within policy. Installed qualification is required before approval
and execution. The model cannot accept residual risk, relax policy, forge a
waiver, reconstruct approval after restart, commit capital or certify compliance.
Changed sources/policy invalidate earlier calculation and approval. Private
record/replay/revocation and retained-context recovery must be backend enforced;
native ADK confirmation alone grants no business authority.

### Research and fixture authorship

Primary sources inspected on 2026-11-09:

- HM Treasury, [The Orange Book](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/orange-book/the-orange-book-management-of-risk-principles-and-concepts):
  explicit assumptions, scenario/dependency analysis, uncertainty and accountable
  human ownership. This general governance guidance is not an oilfield probability
  calibration, legal opinion or a source of universal numeric risk scores.
- Government Analysis Function, [AQuA Book, 2025 edition](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-aqua-book):
  proportionate verification/validation, traceable assumptions and independent
  reference cases. Retain a clear distinction between mathematical verification
  and whether a model is suitable for a real asset.
- [NumPy 2.5 quantile documentation](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/reference/generated/numpy.quantile.html):
  weighted empirical CDF and explicit inverted-CDF quantile convention. Use a
  separately pinned/reference calculation to check frozen expected values;
  a runtime NumPy dependency is not required by the TypeScript backend.
