# How Risk Analyst works
A risk owner supplies three exports: what could go wrong, the owner's limits, and the complete set of assumed joint outcomes. Each outcome states its probability weight, which hazards occur together and its total residual loss. This avoids silently adding overlapping consequences.

The employee discovers the task and loads exact source detail when needed. Risk Analysis checks source rights, bytes, revisions, units and horizon before calculating expected loss, loss quantiles and downside probability. It derives correlations from the declared joint states and keeps zero-variance correlation unavailable.

Missing evidence, conflicts, unknown severity, unmitigated severe hazards and exceeded limits remain explicit blockers. Even an assumed probability of zero cannot erase a severe register entry.

The employee retains permitted source/work references in its own context and proposes a review packet. A separate enrolled human inspects the exact sources, policy, assumptions, work and evaluation revisions. Changes require fresh work; rejection cannot become approval. Installed technical qualification and human approval are both required before a private record.

The calculations describe declared assumptions. Real-asset calibration, materiality, professional risk acceptance and capital decisions remain human responsibilities.
