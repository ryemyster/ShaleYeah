## Contracts and authority

The backend validates supplied identities, units, exact rational arithmetic, evidence/coverage and non-waivable exceptions. The employee contributes `version,summary,observations` with exact original UTF-8 citations. Final output is canonical `work` and `drilling` or a JSON deferral. Supplied engineering and applicability claims are operator-attested; normalization/citations do not establish mechanical sufficiency, safety or complete costs.

A separate authenticated enrolled engineer inspects, requests changes, rejects or approves the exact source/work/packet/context/qualification revision. Installed shared review state enforces expiry, revision/revocation, restart and idempotency. Native confirmation is not approval. Protected `record_drilling_packet` writes only to private-drilling-packets. No spud, AFE/capital commitment, field operations, well-control, safety certification or professional acceptance follows. Enrollment is operator-attested, not license verification.

Source/task/asset/customer/principal rights are rechecked at selection/read/export/dispatch. Successful exact backend work may enter private review context. Pending references survive restart; credentials, human grants and raw model-output promotion are excluded. Required redacted structured audit is fail-closed. Actual owner-only operator files and normalized public/internal snapshots are supported; vendor-native/paid mappings are unverified.
