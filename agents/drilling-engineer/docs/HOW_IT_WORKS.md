# Prepare a drilling packet

The employee discovers the exact task, well/design, supplied options and reviewed constraints. It reads relevant original evidence sections and obtains citations from `cite_drilling_evidence`; the backend calculates byte offsets and hashes. Program, quoted cost/schedule and hazard/offset views show canonical facts and unresolved exceptions.

The employee proposes `version`, `summary` and observations. Each observation names its revision, required question, kind, statement and exact citations. The backend checks source/question coverage and returns canonical `work` and `drilling`, both unreviewed. Missing or mismatched inputs block readiness; unknown does not become zero or low risk.

An enrolled engineer inspects exact sources, assumptions, proposed analysis, context and installed qualification, then requests changes, rejects or approves. Current exact approval permits one private record. It does not permit field operations, well-control, safety certification, AFE/capital commitment or professional engineering acceptance.
