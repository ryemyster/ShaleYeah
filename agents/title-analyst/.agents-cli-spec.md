# Title Analyst employee specification — #685

## Selected architecture and scope

**Stand-alone Agent with Progressive Disclosure (Skills)**: one native ADK employee reads bounded authorized instrument detail and proposes observations. Installed model/event/context/evaluation/MCP/review helpers own those shared contracts. No package-private protocol or reasoning loop is introduced.

Hierarchy is not selected because this output needs one specialist, not child agents. Graph mode is not selected because the installed shared review workflow owns the protected business transition. Ambient processing needs a separately authorized trigger. Capability-first arbitration adds no value to this bounded adoption. The optional orchestrator remains independent.

The role owns `agents/title-analyst`, matching `servers/title` source/review wiring, installed Title evaluation registration, required CI and affected docs. No paid connector, production deployment or general legal title engine is promised.

## Inputs, output and authority

Three exact operator-attested JSON exports describe examination scope, instrument corpus and interpretation/calculation policy. Keep tract/jurisdiction/party/recording identity, original text, dated records, required revisions, explicit opening holdings, declared predecessor/order, depth scope, transfer share bases and WI/whole burdens. Missing records, duplicate/conflicting claims, unknown terms, over-conveyance, wrong units/scope or post-term uncertainty block readiness. Complete supplied opening balances are required; no omitted owner is invented.

The employee proposes cited observations/questions. The backend supplies canonical exact rational holdings/NRI and coverage, then returns `work` and `title` with unreviewed status and no clearance. Citation validation is byte identity and syntactic coverage, not legal entailment, authentic records or complete county coverage.

An enrolled qualified human can inspect, request changes, reject or approve the exact currently qualified revision. Enrollment is operator-attested authority, not automated licensing. The only protected command is `record_title_packet` to `private-title-packets`. No title clearance, marketability certification, legal opinion, curative execution, filing, transfer, lease acceptance or capital authority follows. Native confirmation cannot approve work.
