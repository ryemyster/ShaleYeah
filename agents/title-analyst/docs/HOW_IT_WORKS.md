# How Title Analyst works

The operator supplies an examination, instrument corpus and explicit policy. The employee discovers current authorized records, reads selected original sections, requests exact ownership/burden/chain/lease views and proposes its own cited observations and questions.

The backend calculates holdings and NRI from supplied opening balances, conveyances and royalty/ORRI bases. A shallow operating-right transfer cannot alter record title or deeper rights. Unknown terms, missing records and conflicts remain exceptions. It validates original quote bytes and returns an unreviewed packet with common work/evidence records.

The employee keeps permitted source/work context for this task and asks a separate human to inspect the exact revision. Installed qualification precedes approval. A shared supervisor resumes private recording after approval; revoked sources or changed revisions prevent stale continuation. Neither arithmetic, byte citations nor a privately recorded packet establishes legal title or professional acceptance.
